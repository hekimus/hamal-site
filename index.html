HAMAL

A desktop helper for a video creator's own short-video workflow.

What HAMAL does

HAMAL is a personal desktop application (macOS) that helps a video creator organize videos edited in DaVinci Resolve, generate captions and cover images, and publish the creator's own short videos to the creator's own social media accounts (TikTok and Instagram).

  • Videos are uploaded directly from the user's computer to the platform's official API.
  • HAMAL never publishes content on behalf of other users or third parties.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the local machine and are never shared.
