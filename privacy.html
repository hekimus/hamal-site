Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

1. Summary

HAMAL is a desktop application that runs entirely on your own computer. We do not collect, store, or receive any of your personal data on any server. There is no HAMAL backend, no analytics, and no tracking.

2. What is stored, and where

  • Platform access tokens (TikTok, Instagram): stored only in a local configuration file on your computer (~/.config/hamal/). They are used solely to call the official platform APIs on your behalf and are never transmitted anywhere else.
  • Videos, captions, and cover images: kept in folders on your computer. Videos are uploaded directly from your computer to the platform's official servers when you choose to publish.
  • Publishing records: a local log of successful posts so the same video is not posted twice.

3. What is shared with platforms

When you connect an account, HAMAL uses the official TikTok Login Kit (OAuth) and Content Posting API, and/or the Instagram/Meta Graph API. The following data is exchanged directly between your computer and the platform:

  • Your OAuth authorization (access and refresh tokens).
  • The video file, title/caption, and privacy settings for posts you initiate.
  • Basic creator/account information needed to confirm the correct destination account.

That data is governed by the respective platform's own privacy policy.

4. What we never do

  • We never see, copy, or store your tokens, videos, or account data on any external server.
  • We never share data with third parties or advertisers.
  • We never post without your action in the app.

5. Deleting your data

Because everything is local, deleting the local configuration folder (~/.config/hamal/) removes all tokens and records from your machine. You can also revoke HAMAL's access at any time in your TikTok account settings ("Manage apps") or Instagram authorized apps.
